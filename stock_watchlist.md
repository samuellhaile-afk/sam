## How to Use This Watchlist

1. **Check weekly** — review price changes and any major news for each stock.
2. **Look for setups** — is any stock showing a buy signal from your strategy?
3. **Do not chase** — if a stock already ran up 20% this week, wait for a pullback.
4. **Remove stocks** — if a company's fundamentals change (bad earnings, scandal), remove it.
5. **Add stocks** — found a promising company? Research it first, then add it here.

## What to Watch For Each Stock

| Signal | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Stock near 52-week high | Strong momentum — possible breakout |
| Stock near 52-week low | Could be a value opportunity OR a falling knife — investigate why |
| Earnings report coming | Prices can move big — be careful around earnings dates |
| Moving average crossover | Potential buy or sell signal (see trading strategy) |
| Heavy volume spike | Big investors are moving in or out — pay attention |
